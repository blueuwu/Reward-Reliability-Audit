## Deviation template

For future deviations, use this form before relying on the deviation:

- **Deviation**: what was done differently.
- **Reason**: why the specified behavior could not be followed.
- **Affected acceptance criterion**: section and checklist item.
- **Validation evidence**: command, file, or result proving the deviation holds.
